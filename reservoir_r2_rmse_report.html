Model performance: R² & RMSE

Predicting June–July mean storage (acre-feet) from antecedent Oct-1 storage and May–June inflow. Higher R² and lower RMSE mean a better out-of-sample fit.

Two metrics, two meanings. R² is the unitless share of variance explained on the held-out years (≤1; negative means the model did worse than just predicting the average). RMSE is the typical prediction error in acre-feet — same units as storage — so it is never a fraction. All values are OLS on the test set.
